How It Works

Structured AI deliberation

Three steps. Multiple rounds. One answer you can trust.

1

Ask once

Type your question, upload your documents, and choose which models participate. Your starting model generates the first response.

2

Models critique each other

The other models receive the response and tear it apart. They identify weaknesses, challenge assumptions, flag risks, and propose alternatives. Then the original model revises.

3

Get a battle-tested answer

After multiple rounds of back-and-forth, the system synthesizes everything into a final answer that's been validated from every perspective. Consensus, disagreements, and confidence — all transparent.

Features

Custom Review Focus

Tell the models exactly what to look for. Legal sufficiency, factual accuracy, bias, code quality — or add your own criteria.

Document Upload

Attach up to 10 files. PDFs, code, images, spreadsheets — uploaded natively to each model for deep analysis.
